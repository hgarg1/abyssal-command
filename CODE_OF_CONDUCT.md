# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific when giving feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publication of private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual is publicly representing the project.
